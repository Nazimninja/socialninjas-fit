LEGAL & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At Fit Ninja (fit.socialninjas.in), we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, store, process, and protect your personal profile information, workout logs, and progress tracking files.

1. Information We Collect

To generate your custom fitness programs and nutrition plans, we collect the following information during onboarding and check-ins:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Physical Metrics: Your height, current weight, age, activity level, biological gender, and target fitness goal.
  • Dietary Preferences: Vegetarian, vegan, eggetarian, or non-vegetarian preferences and macronutrient splits.
  • Progress Photos: Photos uploaded to monitor muscle-building or body recomposition progress.
  • Workout Logs: Your logged weights, exercise sets, reps, and workout completion timestamps.

2. How We Use and Process Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to power the coaching and progress algorithms inside our application:

  • Calculating daily caloric requirements and precise macro distribution.
  • Calibrating custom meal plans and portion size scaling.
  • Suggesting barbell/dumbbell training programs with matching target weights and progressive overload cues.
  • Calculating periodic recovery states and check-in progressions.

3. Data Storage and Fail-safe Protection

We leverage secure database systems (Supabase) to store and retrieve your account details with row-level security. To ensure a fast and resilient experience, progress photos are compressed locally using in-memory scaling before uploading to secure storage buckets. If network connectivity is lost in underground gyms, your logs and data are backed up in your browser's encrypted local storage namespace so your progress records are never lost.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal health data with third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing affiliates. Your profile details, gym logs, and progress records remain strictly confidential to your account.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can access, modify, or delete your account information directly from your Profile settings inside the app. You can cancel your coaching subscription at any time with zero penalty.
